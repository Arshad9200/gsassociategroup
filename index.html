About Us

Founded in 2022 in New Delhi, India, GS Associates Group is a leading provider of heavy machinery rental services. We specialize in offering high-quality equipment for construction projects, including concrete mixers, cranes, rollers, and other industrial machinery.

We focus on building long-term relationships with our clients by providing customized solutions tailored to their requirements. Leveraging the latest technology, we ensure timely delivery and top-notch service while maintaining budget-friendly pricing.

Under the guidance of our director, Mahboob Farogh Ahmad, GS Associates Group has grown into a trusted partner for contractors and businesses across India.
